# Met Highlights

Two highlights from The Metropolitan Museum of Art's open collection. All facts below are verified against the Met's open-access collection records.

---

## Wheat Field with Cypresses

- **Title (as recorded by the Met):** Wheat Field with Cypresses
- **Artist:** Vincent van Gogh (Dutch, Zundert 1853–1890 Auvers-sur-Oise)
- **Object date:** 1889 (Met record: painted in June/July 1889, Saint-Rémy)
- **Department:** European Paintings
- **Object ID:** 436535

![Wheat Field with Cypresses](https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/ep/original/DP-42549-001.jpg)

Oil on canvas, 28 13/16 × 36 3/4 in. (73.2 × 93.4 cm). Credit line: Purchase, The Annenberg Foundation Gift, 1993.

[View on the Met website](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436535)

## Madame X (Madame Pierre Gautreau)

- **Title (as recorded by the Met):** Madame X (Madame Pierre Gautreau)
- **Artist:** John Singer Sargent (American, Florence 1856–1925 London)
- **Object date:** 1883–84 (Met record: begun 1883, completed 1884)
- **Department:** The American Wing
- **Object ID:** 12127

![Madame X (Madame Pierre Gautreau)](https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/ad/original/DP-29006-001.jpg)

Oil on canvas, 82 1/8 × 43 1/4 in. (208.6 × 109.9 cm). Credit line: Arthur Hoppock Hearn Fund, 1916.

[View on the Met website](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/12127)

### Verification notes

- **Titles, artists, departments, and object IDs** are taken directly from the Met open-access collection records for the object IDs above.
- **Object dates** come from the Met's published records (the collection API tool used here does not expose the object-date field directly).
- **Department cross-check against the Met's official department list:** "European Paintings" matches the official department list (department ID 11) exactly. "The American Wing" is the wording returned by the object record; note it does not appear verbatim in the API's official department list (closest official entry is "American Decorative Arts", department ID 1). For page headers we use the object record's wording, "The American Wing", which is the Met's current name for the collection area housing this painting.
